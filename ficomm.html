01

Where the money comes from

Blue & Gold clubs can receive two kinds of money. They come from different offices and follow different rules, so it helps to know which one you’re spending.

Through ESC

ASUC Program Funds

Also called “ESC funds.” This is what FiComm awards.

  • Blue & Gold clubs are sponsored, but not funded, by the ASUC. ESC allocates the ASUC’s ABSA funding to them.
  • Can only be spent through the ESC Finance page on Callink.
  • ESC treasurers review every purchase request.
  • Comes with ASUC spending rules. No food, no travel outside the Bay Area, and more.
  • Allocations that aren’t used are returned.

Directly from ESS

ESS Funds

Miscellaneous money ESS puts in your club’s own Callink account.

  • Doesn’t expire at the end of the year.
  • No ASUC spending restrictions.
  • Best for retreats, socials, food, and anything else ASUC funds can’t cover.
  • ESC isn’t responsible for these funds. Ask ESS: Luis Castillo.

One pot, three rounds

The ASUC gives ESC $65,000 in ABSA funds for 2026–27. ESC hands it out in three rounds over the year. FiComm reviews requests for money beyond what a club already has.

  1. Round 1 Initial Allocation Every eligible club’s starting budget for the year. Initial Allocations
  2. Round 2 Fall Allocation Extra funding, requested by presenting to FiComm.
  3. Round 3 Spring Allocation Another FiComm round for spring projects and events.

02

Who FiComm funds

FiComm money is a Blue & Gold benefit. ESC is the student government of the College of Engineering, and it passes ASUC program funds on to Blue & Gold certified engineering organizations. If your club isn’t certified yet, start there.

ESC allocates

Splits the year’s ABSA funds into the initial, fall, and spring rounds.

FiComm decides

Hears club presentations and approves requests for additional funding.

Treasurers review

Check every Callink purchase request against ASUC Finance guidelines.

ESS partners

Runs Blue & Gold with ESC and funds clubs directly with ESS funds.

How FiComm meets

Who’s on it
ESC members from the College of Engineering.
When
Once a week on Fridays, for about an hour. The time can change, so confirm when you sign up.
How you get on the agenda
Sign up to present your request (see step 2 below).
When you hear back
Within one week, by email.

Spend your initial allocation first. If your club still has initial allocation money, use it before asking FiComm for more.

ASUC Program Funds may not be used to:

  1. Purchase food and beverages, with no exemptions or waivers whatsoever for individual, single-use water bottles.
  2. Pay registration fees for intramural teams.
  3. Purchase or reimburse a group for flyers (advertising) that do not display the words “Sponsored by the ASUC” or “ASUC.”
  4. Purchase or reimburse a group for flyers (advertising) that do not indicate the degree of wheelchair accessibility to the event described by the flyer.
  5. Purchase or reimburse a group for stationery that does not display the logo of the ASUC.
  6. Travel outside the Bay Area, defined as the nine counties of Alameda, Contra Costa, Marin, Napa, San Francisco, San Mateo, Santa Clara, Solano, and Sonoma. This does not apply to travel to and from the ASUC Cal Lodge retreat center in Nevada County, CA, while it is under ASUC ownership. The ASUC Senate Finance Committee tells groups applying for travel funding about the benefits and costs of reserving the Cal Lodge.
  7. Pay for any costs related to local events that fall outside the boundaries of Ashby to the south, MLK to the west, Cedar to the north, and the hills to the east.
  8. Pay for any prizes and gifts for individuals.
  9. Purchase alcoholic beverages or illegal substances.
  10. Fund off-campus political activities, such as local, state, or national political campaigns, candidates, or ballot initiatives. This doesn’t stop the ASUC from funding educational initiatives or publicizing positions taken by the Senate, but neither may ask the public to adopt a specific position.
  11. Fund any activity or group that discriminates in recruitment or membership, as described in Article VI, Section 7, Subsection C of the ASUC Constitution, or on the basis of gender identity, immigration status, mental ability, or socioeconomic status.
  12. Pay insurance premiums for hazard level 2 and 3 events on campus.
  13. Buy non-essential latex products such as balloons and rubber bands. Contraceptives and personal protective equipment don’t count as non-essential.
  14. Buy single-use styrofoam and polystyrene products.

06

Getting reimbursed

All ESC funds, including FiComm awards, are paid out through purchase requests (PRs) on Callink. Here’s the path a request takes.

  1. 1Join the ESC page on Callink (accept the treasurers’ invitation).
  2. 2Make a purchase request on the ESC Finance tab.
  3. 3ESC treasurers review it and send it to Stage 2.
  4. 4ASUC Finance reviews it and asks for anything missing.
  5. 5At Stage 5, the money is paid out by the ASUC.

Every request needs

  • A receipt. A photo or digital copy showing the last 4 digits of the card used.
  • How you’ll be paid. Direct deposit, a mailed check, or a check picked up at 432A Eshleman.
  • Payee contact info, including their UID from the Berkeley directory. A UID is 6 or 7 digits and usually starts with 1. Don’t use a student ID or Social Security number.
  • The right payee. An individual or a department, with the same name as on the receipt.
  • A purchase that follows the ASUC spending rules.

Filing it on Callink, step by step

  1. Sign in to Callink

    Go to callink.berkeley.edu and sign in with CalNet.

  2. Open ESC’s page and choose Finance

    Search for Engineering Student Council. The Finance tab only appears after you accept the invitation from the ESC treasurers.

  3. Click Create Request

    The same page shows your club’s past requests, their status, and comments from ASUC Finance. A past treasurer recorded a video walkthrough (Berkeley sign-in required).

  4. Fill in request details and payee info

    Leave nothing blank, or ESC or the ASUC will have to follow up and your money will be delayed. Most requests are reimbursements or credit card requests. For the account, choose:

    3-40-201032-00000-ASUCFunds-ESC

    Its description reads “ASUC Student Fee Funding,” and its parent account is SUMMARY-201032-Engineering Student Council.

  5. Choose the expenditure action

    Double-check the total, the account, the payee, and the expenditure action. If you’re unsure about anything, ask an ESC treasurer instead of guessing.

  6. Add expense info and submit

    Attach the receipt and say when the purchase happened, then submit. An ESC treasurer takes it from there.

Your club’s own Callink account works the same way, except a treasurer with agent training can move requests to Stage 2 and submit them to ASUC Finance directly.

Mistakes that slow requests down

  • Wrong account. Picking “ESC misc” or the summary account instead of the ASUC Program account above.
  • Missing proof of payment. No payee name, or no last 4 card digits on the receipt or bank statement.
  • Wrong payment category. For example, Purchase Order when it should be Vendor.
  • Mismatched payee. The name on the receipt doesn’t match the payee on the request.

Timing

Most reimbursements take 2–3 weeks. If a request sits at Stage 3, contact ASUC Finance at ASUCFINANCE@berkeley.edu.

Hard deadline

Submit every purchase request by the week before finals in the spring semester. Requests after that won’t be approved.

07

Questions

Can we buy food for our events?

Not with ESC or FiComm funds. ASUC program funds never cover food or drinks. Use your ESS funds, which have no ASUC restrictions.

Does unused money roll over to next year?

ESC allocations that aren’t used are returned. ESS funds don’t expire at the end of the year.

Can FiComm fund a trip to a competition?

Only if the travel stays inside the nine Bay Area counties, or goes to the ASUC Cal Lodge. Anything farther needs another source, such as ESS funds or sponsors.

We still have initial allocation money. Can we apply anyway?

Please spend your initial allocation first. FiComm is for funding beyond what your club already has.

Who can be paid back?

The payee can be an individual or a department. The name on the request has to match the receipt, and current Berkeley students, faculty, and staff need their UID from the Berkeley directory.

Where can we see what FiComm approved before?

Past FiComm decisions and initial allocations are on Finance Decisions. Training material is on Treasurer Training.
