Engineering Student Council Committees

Executive Leadership

The presidents lead ESC and coordinate the work of the executive board. The Senior Advisor supports continuity and provides guidance.

Leads: Martha Martinez and Daniel Apolinar, Co-Presidents; Evan Wong, Senior Advisor

External Vice President Committee

Builds relationships with engineering organizations, College of Engineering partners, and other external groups, and supports programs serving affiliated clubs.

Lead: Gilbert Shen, External Vice President

Internal Vice President Committee

Advocates for student organizations and the engineering student body, and works to strengthen connections between students and campus departments.

Leads: Anqi Tao and Nicole Guan, Co-Internal Vice Presidents

Finance Committee

Manages funding for Blue & Gold organizations through allocation processes and funding requests, and supports clubs with financial resources.

Leads: Josemit Hernandez, Senior Treasurer; Ly-Christopher Chhim, Junior Treasurer

Communications Committee

Shares ESC news and events, manages outreach and social media, and creates visual content for the engineering community.

Leads: Belinda Nava and Yitzel Gutierrez, Co-Communications Directors

Engineers' Week Committee

Plans Engineers' Week in collaboration with corporate sponsors, student organizations, and College of Engineering partners.

Lead: Yulia Yang, E-Week Director

Technical Committee

Maintains and develops ESC's websites, applications, and other technology systems.

Lead: Adriel Becerra, Technical Director
