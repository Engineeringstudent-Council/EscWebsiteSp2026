Engineering Student Council

For over 30 years, ESC has been responsible for handling and disbursing ASUC funds to engineering student organizations. We also help run the College of Engineering's Blue & Gold certification program as well as one of the largest engineering themed events on campus: Engineers' Week (E-Week).

Our mission is to represent the engineering student body, support engineering groups in reaching their full potential, and promote a diverse community through successful collaboration between students, faculty, groups, and the College of Engineering.
